Moving nodes is easy.

I put that "Tutorial Window" inside another window node. But I personally don't like putting windows inside each other. So how about we clean that up now?

There are three ways to move nodes or branches around:

  • Drag & Drop: Grab that "Tutorial Window" node with the mouse. Put the mouse cursor over it, press and hold the left mouse button, then move the mouse cursor around. It should let you drag the node to a new location, with the destination indicated by some extra markers in the tree structure.

    Drag to the right side of a node to place the dragged item as a child of its target. Or drag to the far left side of a node to place it as a next sibling.

    Try dragging stuff around to get a feel for how it works.
  • Hotkeys: The mouse is great, but if you need to move a long way or move a lot of items, it's kind of "a drag", so to speak. It's slow and tedious and some positions are hard to target precisely. So use the keyboard.

    Click in the sidepanel to focus it, put the panel cursor on the node you want to move, and then hold Shift and press the Arrow keys to move the node around.

    The fastest way to move that "Tutorial Window" out of its original position is to click it once to put the cursor on it, then hold Shift and press Left twice to promote it to the top level.

    Or, if you mapped some keys in the browser's "Extension shortcuts" config, you can use those keys instead... and don't have to focus the sidepanel first. But that's not configured by default. I use Alt+Shift+Arrows for that, but the default is unmapped.
  • Mark & Paste: Whether you use mouse or keyboard, moving nodes one at a time is slow. And if you use "Window" mode in your sidepanels, you can't drag or slide nodes into another window that way. So there is a more powerful way to do it:

    Click the "M" button in the hover menu, or press the "M" key on the keyboard, to mark a node. Mark as many as you like. It should highlight the marked nodes and display a count of how many are marked.

    Then navigate to the place where you want to move them to, make sure the panel cursor is on the destination, and press the "P" key to paste the nodes there. Or click the counter where it shows how many nodes are marked.

    For example, you could pick several items from a list, change to a different window, then paste. This moves all those items and their children to the new window, including any open tabs attached to them.
