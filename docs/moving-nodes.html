Moving nodes is easy.

There are three ways to move nodes or branches around:

  • Drag & Drop: Grab a node with the mouse, press and hold the left mouse button, then move the mouse cursor around. Drag to the right side of a node to place it as a child. Drag to the left side to place it as a next sibling.

  • Hotkeys: Put the cursor on a node in the sidepanel, then hold Shift and press the Arrow keys to move the node around. Use Shift+Left twice to promote a node out of a branch. (Requires Session view mode to move across windows).

  • Mark & Paste: Press M on the keyboard or click the M button to mark a node. Mark as many nodes as you like. Then move to the destination and press P to paste them. Press Shift+M to unmark everything when done.

Change node order

Mark and paste is also a quick way to reorder nodes: mark them in the order you want them to appear, then paste.
